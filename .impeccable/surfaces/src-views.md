# Public site (home + animal listings)

Scope: public site of the Refuge Animalier du Pays de Landerneau (home, animal list, animal plate, adopted album, agenda, news, help, adopt, contact). Visitor mode: Persuade (home, help) and Read (listings, adopt process). The back office inherits the same world in Operate mode.

Audience: future adopters on phone around Landerneau; donors and volunteers. Action: find an animal that fits their home, then apply (form or email for cats in foster care); secondary: donate, volunteer, come to events.

Constraints: sample animals are fictional and have no photos; they are shown as authored illustrations labelled as such until volunteers upload photos. No invented testimonials or figures.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every animal gets a portrait plate: posed alone on a flat painted backdrop, framed by a thin double rule, its character named in a ruled caption beneath (Chats, Chiens, Enfants, Logement, Âge). Refuses the category default of pastel paw prints, rounded photo cards and a guilt-tinted hero photo.

OWN-WORLD: Slate-ink rail and type on a pale shell-pink ground; backdrops in Breton landscape hues: gorse yellow, heather pink, fern green, sea-foam, sky. Tall condensed display serif for names, condensed caps for caption labels, plain sans body. Hairline double-rule frames, ruled caption tables with vertical dividers, a small diamond ornament between rules, arrow-led text links. Status (réservé, SOS, adopté) printed as a caption stamp, never a pill badge.

STORY: The visitor meets one animal at full presence, understands who it gets along with in one glance at the caption, browses the series, then knows exactly how to apply and what it costs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left slate rail with the refuge name and navigation. Center: the featured animal's name set huge in the condensed serif, a one-line character sentence, primary "Voir tous les animaux", secondary "Comment adopter". Right: the featured plate filling the height, backdrop in its hue, ruled caption below; prev/next arrows and a counter "01 / 06". Changing plates changes the backdrop hue.

FORM: Congolese dandy portrait plate (challenger, dealt by the roll, chosen by the user over the assigned Marinière direction); seed key 6bc7c3b8. Signature interaction: plate change animates a backdrop hue wash, the name reveals through a mask, caption cells tick in sequence; visitors can mark a coup de coeur that leaves a printed tick on the plate.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
